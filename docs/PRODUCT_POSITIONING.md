# Product positioning and content discipline

D161 · 22 September 2026

## What should make the experience distinctive

Etail Depot makes financial products easier to understand through familiar customer actions. Trolleys turns a sale into a payment request the merchant can share, with optional purchase recognition after payment. Ledgix brings secured credit to Apple Wallet and Google Wallet, explaining the deposit and repayment as separate responsibilities.

The ambition is a confident new entrant. Express that through specific benefits, consistent interactions and clear commercial terms. Do not claim category invention, market leadership, guaranteed results or an exclusive capability without supporting evidence.

## How the pages work together

- **Home:** introduce the business outcome and the Two Step name; demonstrate bookings, work/expertise and sales; introduce the upcoming Digital Wallet Card with its essential terms. Lead to the appropriate product page.
- **Payments:** Create → Share → customer payment, followed by optional Clarity, one full pricing explanation and practical answers. Keep the same purchase across the artwork. Channels distribute a request; they are not different card networks or additional merchant steps.
- **Cards:** the wallet proposition, deposit/fees/repayment, the separate purchase cycle, wallet setup and practical questions. Keep applications visibly closed until launch.
- **Help and notices:** use the same names and offers, clear support/access directions and accurate website-versus-product responsibilities.

## Commercial and visual consistency

Trolleys Two Step remains 2.9% + 30¢ per successful domestic online card payment, with no monthly fee. Clarity is separately optional at 0.4% of eligible enrolled successful-payment volume. Ledgix retains the selected $200 minimum deposit, $0 annual/monthly fees and full monthly repayment. Final card eligibility, limits, other charges and deposit return remain subject to the forthcoming agreement.

Keep the company header/footer, ink/paper colors, section widths, typography and controls consistent. Illustrations can have distinct product treatments while sharing proportions, readable labels and deliberate spacing. Decorative examples never become live financial controls.
