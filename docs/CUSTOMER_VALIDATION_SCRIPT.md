# Customer-task validation — D132

Prepared 21 September 2026. No participants or results are claimed in this file.

Use five prospective merchants and five card prospects for formative sessions. After corrections, validate with ten fresh participants per cohort. Include phone users and people who use assistive technology. Obtain consent for any recording; do not ask for financial account details or perform a real application. Compare the deployed PR #30 baseline with the local candidate using equivalent tasks, alternating order to reduce familiarity effects.

## Opening

Ask about the participant’s most recent real need to collect a payment or choose a card, what they used and what made the decision difficult. Do not describe Etail’s intended advantage first.

Show the homepage for 15 seconds, then ask: “What is this website offering, and what can you use today?” Record their words without correcting them. Ask where they would go next for their own need.

## Merchant tasks

1. “You need a customer to pay an $80 booking amount. Show how this service would help.” Observe whether the participant understands the request/share/customer-payment sequence and distinguishes artwork from actual checkout.
2. “A domestic online card payment is $100. How much would remain after the fees shown? What changes if you invoice?” Target explanation: $96.80; $96.40 with the stated invoice fee, before other applicable charges.
3. “Your customer says they have paid. Where would you check, and when can you treat the money as in your bank?” Look for distinction between payment confirmation, availability and bank arrival; no guaranteed same-day answer.
4. “Where would you find out about a refund, dispute or business eligibility?” Record whether the fee disclosure, application review and support paths are found without prompting.
5. “Show where you would start an application. What do you expect to happen after this link?” Stop before sending any external application data.

## Card-prospect tasks

1. “What kind of card is this, and can you apply now?” Target: virtual Visa secured credit, coming soon.
2. “What money is needed up front, and what account fees are listed?” Target: $200 minimum deposit; $0 annual/monthly fees; other terms still need the final agreement.
3. “If you make purchases, how is that bill paid? Does the deposit pay it?” Look for separate debt and repayment, full monthly repayment, optional early payments.
4. “How would you add the card to your phone?” Observe independent discovery of the guide, Apple/Google choice and setup/verification distinction.
5. “What would you still need to know before choosing this card? Where would you ask?” Record unresolved eligibility, limit/deposit, return and agreement concerns in the participant’s own words.

## Record and acceptance

For each task record device, completion without help, elapsed time, hesitation/misunderstanding, recovery and exact comment. No invented scores or filled sample participants. Give a neutral prompt only after recording an unaided failure. Separate satisfaction from correct task completion.

For a fresh validation group, the D131 target is at least 9/10 identifying offer/availability after 15 seconds, finding the next step within 30 seconds and correctly explaining key costs/money rules within 60 seconds. Record visual, navigation, mobile and decision-confidence scores separately with the evidence behind each. Unresolved material misunderstanding blocks a confident 9/10 claim regardless of attractive artwork. These small sessions diagnose problems; they do not establish population conversion rates or product-market fit.
