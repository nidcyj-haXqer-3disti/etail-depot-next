## What is implemented

- Seller master merchant contexts; Personal and Household sub-merchant collection units. One immutable master per sub-unit; multiple separate Seller associations per host; one unit per master/host pair. The confirmed model places sales, fees, refunds and chargeback responsibility with the named master Seller.
- Explicit request, independent host/Seller acceptance and suspension. Current controller authority and acceptance versions are checked again for operations and retries. A stored acceptance does not become provider verification.
- Household merchant-only invitations for named members or outside helpers. The existing invitation lifecycle dispatches by typed account/merchant target. Accepted assignments have explicit permissions, onward-delegation ceilings, expiry, optional continued host-membership dependency, current ancestry and revocation. Merchant assignments create no Personal, Household or Seller account membership.
- One **Payment channels** form and canonical command for SMS/text, email, invoice, bill, social, QR and website-link presentations. Exact USD draft amounts, one-time/reusable intent, expiry, review/save/edit/close, private or explicitly shared visibility, stale-version recovery and lost-response/HTTP408 retry are supported. A receipt is described as following a confirmed payment; no receipt or payable invoice is fabricated.
- Merchant directory, merchant-only navigation, People & permissions, and Verification & gateway. Requirements distinguish local authority/consent from Seller legal identity, business and representative review; Personal controller identity; Household controller/guardian authority and merchant delegation; external MID/gateway; payment acceptance, payouts and delivery.
- Existing canonical Seller creation/legal-setup forms, issuing/card controls and account experiences remain in place. Deprecated draft HTTP routes are removed; direct compatibility test helpers forward to the same channel writer. Existing physical draft records and IDs are retained.
