# D137 — Trolleys Sale Records and the Payments page

21 September 2026. The user accepts researching Order Insight and rebuilding the public Payments page with one optional +0.4% Trolleys service. This supersedes D133–D136’s independent enhanced receipt, invoice and agreement add-ons. Existing base processing and card terms remain unchanged.

## Product and price

**Trolleys Sale Records** organizes the agreed purchase, payment, actual fulfillment and supporting history. Goods, Services and Information remain distinct and may coexist in a real sale. Information retains subject/scope, timing, freshness, promised standard, access/use and delivery evidence; it is not reduced to a file format.

The approved direction is a **Trolleys fee of 0.4% of eligible enrolled successful-payment volume plus additional vendor charges**, separate from processing. It is not a 0.4% uplift on the supplier invoice, an inclusive vendor tariff or a fee per dispute won. On $1,000 the Trolleys component is $4. Basic payment receipts remain included without the optional service. Invoice, agreement and receipt are documents within the sale record, not individually billed add-ons.

Vendor price, arrangement, coverage, integration and enrollment are unconfirmed. The product is coming soon. [Primary-source research](research/d137-order-insight.md) distinguishes Order Insight purchase recognition and qualifying fraud deflection from RDR/CDRN credit-resolution and post-chargeback representment. Trolleys does not claim authorized-reseller status or white-label Order Insight. Before representing this as an authorized or operational offering, confirm the contracted supplier requirements, including any required marketing approval.
