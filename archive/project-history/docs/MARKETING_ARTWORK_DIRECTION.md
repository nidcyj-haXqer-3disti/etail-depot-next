## Direction for review

Keep the restored homepage copy, section order, dimensions, header and footer. Improve the hero artwork as one focused creative step rather than another page redesign.

**Proposed scene:** a creative business owner hands a neatly wrapped, finished order to a customer across a worktable. A colleague quietly finishes another order in the background. The foreground exchange is the focal point; natural expressions provide warmth. Avoid a group posing around a phone.

Use soft window light, warm ivory, natural wood/paper, realistic skin tones and restrained forest-green details. Compose for the existing desktop frame and a tighter mobile crop, retaining the hands, finished order and human interaction in both. Keep the scene uncluttered, without headlines, floating interfaces, card renders or channel icons baked into the photograph. In a future integration, the adjacent copy should carry the explanation; the image should not repeat it through multiple slogans and feature pills.

The satisfaction of finishing work and serving a customer supports “Get paid. Keep going.” The existing Ledgix section already introduces “For your family. For your business.” There is no need to illustrate cards, family and payments simultaneously in the hero. Do not imply that pictured people are actual customers, relatives or endorsers, or invent product outcomes.
