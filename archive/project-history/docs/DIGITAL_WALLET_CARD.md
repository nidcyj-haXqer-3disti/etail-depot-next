## Product identity and boundaries

Digital Wallet Card is the customer-facing product name. Its underlying financial type remains a **virtual Visa secured credit card**. A security deposit backs the credit account; purchases create a separate balance to repay. This presentation change does not create a prepaid balance, alter repayment terms, open applications, activate wallet provisioning or add an internal account runtime. Coming-soon status and the Ledgix contact remain.

Name the three confirmed wallet options. Do not claim compatibility with every wallet or uniform worldwide acceptance. Apple and Google use card addition/provisioning; WeChat Pay links an eligible international card for supported merchant payments in mainland China. Availability and features depend on device, location and card eligibility. Use ordinary customer words rather than tokenization or processor terminology on the public pages.
